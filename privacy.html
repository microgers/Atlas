Privacy Policy

Atlas collects nothing. This page explains what that means in practice.

The short version. Atlas has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no network requests. Nothing you do in the app is transmitted anywhere, because the app never opens a connection at all.

What Atlas stores

Atlas keeps a record of your study progress so it can schedule reviews: which countries you have answered, how many times you got each one right or wrong, which box each country sits in, and when it is next due. It also remembers your chosen map projection and whether spoken pronunciation is on.

All of this is written to local storage on your own device. It is not an account, it is not backed by a server, and it is not readable by anyone but you.

What Atlas does not do

  • No personal information is requested or collected — no name, email, phone number or age.
  • No identifiers are collected or generated, including advertising identifiers.
  • No location data, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access. The app does not request these permissions because it does not use them.
  • No analytics, telemetry, session recording or usage statistics.
  • No advertising and no advertising networks.
  • No tracking, and no sharing of any data with third parties, because there is no data to share and no third parties involved.

Network access

Atlas ships every country outline, capital, pronunciation and lesson inside the app itself. It works fully in Airplane Mode from the first launch, and it makes no network requests during normal use.

Children

Atlas is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either, and it contains no advertising, no external links, no user-generated content and no in-app purchases.

Deleting your data

Tap Reset all progress at the bottom of the Modules screen to erase everything the app has stored. Deleting the app removes it as well. There is no server-side copy to request, export or delete.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Atlas ever starts collecting anything, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
