Support

Atlas is a geography trainer that works entirely offline. Here is how it works and how to get help.

Need a person? Email micjared.rogers@gmail.com. Say which iPhone or iPad you are on, which version of iOS, and what happened. Replies usually come within a few days.

How Atlas works

Pick a module, then a lesson. Atlas shows you where the region sits, introduces each country with its capital, pronunciation and a memory hook, then asks you to find each one on the map with no labels. It mixes in countries from earlier lessons so nothing slips away.

A country is only counted as mastered after you have produced it from memory several times, spread out over days. That is deliberate: recalling something after a delay is what makes it stick, and it is why the app makes you wait rather than letting you grind a region in one sitting.

Practice modes

  • Lessons — the guided path through a module, region by region.
  • Quiz — endless mixed questions over a module: find it, name it, name the capital, match the pronunciation.
  • Silhouettes — one country, no borders, no neighbours, no labels.
  • Speed round — sixty seconds, as many as you can.
  • Review — whatever the spacing schedule says is due today.

Common questions

Where does the voice come from?

Every country, capital and region name was recorded ahead of time with a neural voice and ships inside the app, so it sounds identical on every device and needs no connection. Your phone's own text-to-speech is never used unless a recording is somehow missing.

The pronunciation button is silent.

Check that the iPhone silent switch is off and the volume is up. Atlas plays speech through the ambient audio channel, so it will not interrupt music, but it also respects the silent switch. You can also turn spoken audio off entirely under Modules → Pronunciation audio.

My progress disappeared.

Progress lives on the device only. It is lost if you delete the app, reset progress from the Modules screen, or erase website data for the app. There is no account and therefore no way to restore it from a server.

Does it sync between my iPhone and my iPad?

No. Each device keeps its own progress, because nothing is sent off the device.

A country I need for class is missing.

Atlas covers the 196 countries used across the eleven regional modules. If one you need is not there, email the module and the country and it can go into the next update.

Can I use it without a connection?

Yes, always. The whole map and every lesson are inside the app. There is nothing to download and nothing to sign in to.
